It is package test input and review prose, not SQL, a runtime limiter, authorization policy or evidence that a query has run. Each row names a module, qualified selector, category, SQL/cardinality explanation, consumer kind and concrete consumer description. Repeated selectors additionally identify the call's ordered occurrence. No executable validation accompanied this note.

Examples matter more than a category label. Overview plan/approval/receipt queries use ambiguity sentinels; overview_runs examines at most 101 candidates, returning up to 100 records or None on overflow. Receipt selection prefers incomplete admissions, otherwise the latest completions, and retains ambiguity rather than inferring successful execution. Revision history candidates have the smaller eleven-row maximum, while ordinary pages generally select limit plus one. Descending probe timelines and ascending metadata/temporal/ordinal pages have different seek contracts. The [paging language](src/control_plane_kit_operations/read_pages.py.md) and actual selector/projection remain authoritative.

Internal-complete is not synonymous with test-only. It includes operator intent and execution history, verification keys, active runtime/ingress/product catalogues, provider/reference supersession chains, generated ingress custody and compensation evidence. Other complete selectors are explicitly attributed to tests. Naming a complete consumer records why truncation would be wrong; it does not certify its memory use, query cost, provider truth or authorization. Likewise, exact-verifier includes aggregates, full scans in keyset batches and observation-count-bounded reconstruction. Bounded individual reads do not imply bounded total work or bounded aggregate response bytes. Saved-preparation verification says that explicitly.

The principal consumer is [test_postgres_read_cardinality](tests/test_postgres_read_cardinality.py.md). These are syntax/accounting checks, not resolution of consumer references, SQL semantics or a transitive proof that every read is bounded. Other read forms, aliases and non-postgres modules are outside that census.
