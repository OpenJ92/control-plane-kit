# Admitted management capability dispatch

These targets reuse actual PostgreSQL permission, management assignment, start,
coordinator and invocation owners. The controlled health adapter records the
owner-supplied selection and changes the assignment through its normal command
service after admission. Initial native A must stay A after regrant B; a newly
admitted successor B must stay B after regrant A. Signed dispatch carries A and
the next semantic unit refuses after a distinct-capability regrant. Existing
event, request fingerprint, outcome and replay correlation remain asserted.

This is owner handoff evidence, not Docker construction or physical acquisition
proof. Servers owns mapping the handed-off selection to its installed catalog.
Target-red and implementation validation are pending under #2055.
