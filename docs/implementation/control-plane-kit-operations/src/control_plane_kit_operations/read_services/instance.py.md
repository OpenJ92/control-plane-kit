InstanceReadService is the public composition facade over private read projections. Workspace and graph stores are required; history, execution, observation, authority, secret, gateway, draft and saved-history stores are optional dependencies passed to their owners. Construction wires these objects rather than opening a UnitOfWork or checking all optional-store availability eagerly. A method needs the stores its projection actually uses; absence of an unrelated store need not block other reads.

Methods forward to workspace/graph, operation history, observation freshness, authority/secret, gateway, draft/history and operator-overview owners. Collection methods carry ReadPageRequest through unchanged; scalar details remain separate from child pages. Operator overview has its own limit/after interface (default 50), not a global pagination limit. Return models/descriptors, membership, errors and bounds are defined by the delegated owners and their stores, not reimplemented by this facade.

The configured graph codec goes to workspace/graph and operation-history projections. Draft detail instead uses its own DEFAULT_GRAPH_CODEC contract; overriding this facade's codec is not a universal decoder replacement. The injected clock and freshness policy go only to observation projection; no one-instant snapshot across all read methods follows from supplying a clock. Caller transaction/snapshot consistency remains outside the facade.

Workspace admission callbacks are shared with several projections, but workspace existence is not actor authorization. The authenticated CpkServerReadService adapter owns that outer boundary and its request UnitOfWork. Direct facade calls take no principal and cannot prove a token, grant or permission. This module itself commits nothing, invokes no provider, resolves no secret and approves or executes no action.

Read descriptors are not uniformly secret-scrubbed by composition. Individual owners apply selective redaction and expose their declared public fields; stored comments, plan descriptors or fixed/dynamic errors must be assessed at those owners. Plan-detail recovery candidates and overview next-action values describe existing planning/history semantics, not autonomous compensation, recovery or new authority. Stored observations and histories are not freshly observed provider truth.
